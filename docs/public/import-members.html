Sacrament Meeting Planner

Import your member list

English Português Español

Fill your ward's member list in minutes. You'll gather two files — the ward member list (PDF) and the app's CSV — and let an AI assistant merge them into one clean CSV you upload back into the app. The AI handles name order, phone formatting, children's privacy, and careful merging for you.

1 Get the member-list PDF

  1. Open the ward member list on LCR.
  2. Click Print → All members → Print.
  3. Save the PDF to your device.

2 Export the app's CSV

  1. In the app, go to Settings → Member List.
  2. Tap Export CSV.
  3. If you don't have anyone in the app yet, delete the example rows that come in the CSV.
  4. Save the CSV to your device.

No members yet? You can skip this step — the AI will build the CSV from the PDF alone.

3 Generate the final CSV with AI

Copy the prompt below, open your favorite AI, paste it, attach the two files (PDF + CSV), and press Enter. The AI will show a short report — resolve any phone conflicts it lists — and then give you the final CSV.

          Copilot

After clicking, the prompt is on your clipboard. In the AI chat: paste
        (Ctrl/Cmd+V), attach the two files, and press Enter.

        
If the AI lists phone conflicts (same person, different numbers in the app vs.
        the PDF), it will ask you to choose which to keep — decide before it produces the final CSV.

      


      

        
4 Import the CSV into the app

        
    
          
  1. Save/download the final CSV the AI produced.
    2. 
          
  2. In the app, go to Settings → Member List.
    3. 
          
  3. Tap Import CSV, pick the file, review, and confirm.
    4. 
        

        
Importing replaces the app's member list with the CSV — make sure it's the
        final, merged file.
